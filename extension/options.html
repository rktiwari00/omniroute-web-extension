Security & Privacy

The extension only requests access to the chosen domain when you click AUTHORIZE AND COPY. Credentials are processed locally, copied directly to your clipboard, and never sent to external servers.

Clear captured data

Removes temporarily captured authorization headers and cookies stored in Chrome session memory.

Responsible Use & Compliance

Use this extension only with accounts, devices, and systems that belong to you or for which you have explicit authorization. Paste copied credentials immediately into OmniRoute and overwrite the clipboard afterwards.
